Who uses v2?

New runs and restarts use v2 once its launch setting is enabled and the cutover has passed. Earlier runs stay on v1 through their next stages. A v1 trader can accept the new Terms and switch while flat. Switching is permanent: balance, loss state, stage, strikes and held Stage 2 profit carry over; this stage’s v2 counted trades and score start at the switch.

Trading and scoring

Pricing

Spreads use the reference spread and each instrument’s configured markup. If reference quotes are unavailable, the current IPFX spread is used. The same simulated execution model applies to every v2 trader, whether copied or not. Market fills include the existing 0.3–0.9-second execution delay and slippage; stops include market gaps.

Payouts and reviews

Stage 2 profit is held. After Stage 3 completion, the profit share remains 85% of eligible Stage 2 and Stage 3 closed profit, with the existing $500 minimum and $700 cap. Identity verification, a payout method and good standing are required. Stage 4 keeps the existing 85% split, 14-day interval and payout filters. Requests are reviewed and paid manually.

One account per person. Existing linked-account, opposite-side ring and identical-trade evidence pauses advancement and payout for review; trading remains available. A new Stage 1 attempt is available only within the monthly allowance and after any breach lockout.

Accounts are simulated. IPFX may copy or hedge trading for its own risk management; this does not change a v2 trader’s simulated fill. Pricing or scoring errors may be corrected with the change shown on the dashboard.
