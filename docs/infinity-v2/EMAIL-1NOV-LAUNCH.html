Six rules to know
  1. Stop loss on every trade, at least 8× the spread away. Tighten it or move it to break-even, but never further away.
  2. Hold at least 5 minutes for profit to count. Losses always count.
  3. After a stop-out, that instrument pauses for 10 minutes.
  4. Trades on the same instrument and direction within 15 minutes count as one trade.
  5. Your consistency score rises when your wins steadily outweigh your losses. It's on your dashboard live.
  6. One account per person.
