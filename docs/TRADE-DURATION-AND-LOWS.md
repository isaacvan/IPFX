# Trade duration and lowest recorded P&L — 8 October 2026

Team Brain → select a trader → Current IPFX trades now shows **Time open** and **Lowest P&L**. Duration is the saved open-to-exit interval, including seconds; open rows show elapsed time. Invalid/missing timestamps are unavailable. Hovering a duration shows the saved timestamps.

Lowest P&L is the lowest **observed IPFX floating gross USD P&L**, before commission, financing and execution slippage, for the lots displayed on that row. It is distinct from final credited IPFX P&L and from E8 reverse/copy estimates. Buys mark at bid; sells mark at ask. A partial row covers the original open through that partial exit, for the exited lots. The remaining parent covers its remaining lots, so the row lows cannot be summed into an original-position drawdown. All-positive observations remain positive; missing evidence is never converted into a zero loss.

Migration `20261008121001_trade_duration_and_lows.sql` registers future IPFX opens/partial exits, seeds open/recent closed rows from retained quotes, and runs a bounded asynchronous database worker every ten seconds. It reads existing quote ticks, uses no broker/API requests, and preserves each row's low after raw ticks are purged. Reads refresh every ten seconds; quote processing can lag under backlog. Hovering a low shows its observation timestamp and processed-through timestamp, and delayed processing is labelled catching up. The Brain health list includes the worker heartbeat.

Contract size and quote currency freeze at registration. Non-USD conversion uses a recorded contemporaneous USD pair mid, at most sixty seconds old; the matching USD base pair uses its own tick mid. Unknown conversion, invalid/crossed/nonfinite quotes and unsupported specifications remain unavailable or incomplete. A changed source entry price invalidates the displayed low rather than silently repricing old observations.

Raw IPFX ticks retain only six hours and contain recorded feed price changes, not every broker tick. Existing long-lived rows are explicitly partial history; old closed rows with no retained lifetime quotes remain unavailable. A two-second settling delay, thirty-second overlap and thirty-second post-exit settlement allow ordinary late inserts; later missing ticks and feed outages can still make observed lows less adverse than the true intratrade minimum. This is not a guaranteed true market/E8 minimum or verified net P&L. The asynchronous worker has a bounded batch, advisory single-worker lock and timeout configuration; this does not establish launch-scale capacity.

No classifier thresholds, model selections, broker destinations, account balances or challenge terms are changed. E8 remains read-only. SQL tests cover buy/sell spread sides, exact durations, positive minima, retention, currency conversion, invalid quotes, partial cutoffs, durable lows and service-only access; the intercepted browser fixture exercises the rendered columns and missing-history labels.
