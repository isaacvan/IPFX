# Reverse simulation and owner B-live review

Internal E8 estimates reverse a trader in B states (buy becomes sell and sell becomes buy), keeping entry direction frozen through partial/full exits. They use observed E8 bid/ask after the configured delay, account-specific observed contract evidence where available, estimated published commission and adverse-tick scenarios. They are not actual broker orders. Missing quotes, incomplete quantities, unsupported FX, fees or instrument evidence are not fabricated as zero.

The owner Brain now separates early WATCH (existing ten-trade trend window, positive reverse replay average above the policy edge and recent reverse trend, negative same-direction replay) from POLICY_REVIEW (the unchanged classifier actually returns BB_LIVE). The latter uses existing trades/days, lower bound, edge/evidence, concentration, dwell/cool-off, holding and lag guards, including A-model/Stage2 shortcut precedence. Automatic moves and owner overrides remain unchanged. SELECTED means the state is B-live, not that a destination was enabled or filled.

Recommendations are written by the existing classifier and scanned into owner-page alerts. Freshness and active policy/state/integrity checks suppress outdated recommendations. Page pulses are ten seconds; new statistics depend on the existing minute ledger/classifier cycle. Alert solutions open the existing trader/model review or refresh checks; they do not warn or pause a trader for merely being unprofitable, enable accounts or send external notifications.

Open a trader for the criteria checklist and a separate 30-day E8 estimate summary. It counts all closed non-practice Infinity roots and capture coverage, separates frozen A/B roots, and limits expensive cost detail to the latest 200 B roots. Aggregate amounts are unavailable unless every sampled root has a completed compatible estimate; the sample limit, backcasts, size-check gaps and exclusions remain visible. Partial child rows are not counted as new root trades. No sampled gross, cost scenario or E8 quote summary changes classifier evidence or verifies net/cash income.

E8 remains read-only. Physical demo execution, authenticated complete broker receipts, account-specific costs/swaps/slippage and destination/risk/capacity checks are still needed before actual broker net is established. Existing Off/Paper validation controls are a separate journal and do not turn this all-source simulator off. Stage2 +2.75% policy, live allocation/risk and trader challenge rules are unchanged.
