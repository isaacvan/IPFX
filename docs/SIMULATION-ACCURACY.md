These are public schedule estimates, not proof of this account's settings or historical fees. Original undocumented defaults are replaced; custom owner rates remain. Immutable schedule evidence keeps source and observation time. Unknown costs are never measured zeros.

`e8_sim_accuracy` adds a read-only breakdown to the existing owner-only Brain activity RPC. It prefers the specification observed before entry; a later observation is explicitly labelled a historical backcast. USD gross is rebased from the frozen IPFX lot-size assumption to observed E8 units per lot; unsupported conversion stays unknown. Observed min/max/step failures suppress cost-adjusted amounts. The all-in spread comparison likewise uses separate E8 and IPFX contract sizes, requires fresh conversion quotes, and refuses to blend multiple reference accounts into one unsupported contract assumption.

For each trade, **Review calculation** shows event/quote timestamps in UK time, quantities, bid/ask, contract basis, entry/exit sampling waits, decision quote age, commission basis, and adverse one/five-tick scenarios. Partial exits are weighted and commission allocated to exited quantity once. IPFX's captured source commission is subtracted from its decision-quote baseline as a partial cost estimate, never actual reverse execution. Missing/future/stale decision timing prevents an adjusted baseline. The original gross archive remains intact.
