Delete your account

To delete your IPFX Capital account, sign in and choose Delete account at the bottom of the menu on your dashboard. Trading stops straight away and your personal information is permanently deleted after 7 days, unless you cancel. Trading statistics are kept only in anonymised form that cannot be linked to you.

Can't sign in? Email enquiries@ipfxcapital.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. More in our privacy policy.
