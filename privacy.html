1.Who we are

IPFX Capital Ltd ("IPFX", "we", "us", "our") operates ipfxcapital.com and the IPFX Capital Markets trading interface (together, the "Platform"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Platform, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

IPFX Capital Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. This policy covers data protection only — for the terms governing your use of the Platform itself, see our Terms and Conditions, which this Privacy Policy is incorporated into by reference.

2.Data we collect

Account dataName, email address, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), country of residence, referral code.
KYC / identity dataDate of birth, nationality, government-issued ID documents, proof of address — collected before any Profit Split payment is made, as described in our Terms.
Payment dataWe never receive or store your full card number, expiry date, or CVV. Card details are entered directly into forms hosted by our payment processor (Stripe) and go straight to them — we only receive a payment confirmation and a reference ID.
Trading dataEvery order you place, modify, or close on the Platform: instrument, size, entry/exit price, stop loss, take profit, timestamps, and account balance history. See Section 4.
Technical dataIP address, device and browser type, and general usage/log data, collected automatically for security, fraud prevention, and diagnosing issues.
CommunicationsSupport messages, live-chat transcripts, and anything you send us via email or the contact form.

3.How we use your data

  • To create and administer your account and run the challenge/evaluation programme;
  • To process payments and refunds, and to pay out Profit Splits to Qualified Traders;
  • To verify your identity (KYC) and meet our anti-money-laundering obligations;
  • To detect rule violations, prohibited trading patterns, and fraud (including cross-account correlation detection described in our Terms);
  • To provide customer support and respond to enquiries;
  • To send service communications (account, security, payment status) and, only if you opt in, marketing emails;
  • To improve the Platform and understand how it's used, generally in aggregated/anonymised form.

4.Trading data specifically

Our Terms and Conditions (Section 11, "Intellectual Property and Data Rights") grant IPFX a broad licence to use Trading Data generated on your account. We want to be direct about what that can include in practice, beyond internal analytics:

Trade mirroring: IPFX operates an internal feature that can replicate a trader's order activity from their evaluation/funded account onto a separate live trading account that IPFX controls or has an interest in, scaled by a volume multiplier. This is an admin-controlled setting, not something every account has enabled by default. Where it is enabled on your account, your live order decisions are copied — in real time and at your actual size and timing — onto that other account.

We are disclosing this here, plainly, because we think a general "we may use your data" clause is not adequate notice for something this specific. If this section doesn't yet fully and accurately describe how mirroring works for your account, or if you have questions about whether it applies to you, contact us using the details in Section 14 before continuing to trade.

Aggregated or anonymised trading statistics (e.g. platform-wide win rates, instrument popularity) may also be used publicly or shared with third parties without identifying you individually.

5.Our legal basis for processing

Where UK/EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases, depending on the activity: performance of our contract with you (running your account, processing payments); compliance with a legal obligation (KYC/AML record-keeping); our legitimate interests (fraud and rule-violation detection, service improvement) balanced against your rights; and, for marketing emails and the trade-mirroring feature described in Section 4, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by contacting us.

6.Who we share data with

SupabaseOur database, authentication, and backend infrastructure provider. Account, trading, and audit data is stored on Supabase's servers.
StripeOur payment processor once live. Handles card data directly — see Section 2.
TradingViewChart visualisation embedded in the trading interface. TradingView may set its own cookies; see their privacy policy for details.
MetaApi / broker connectionsUsed to execute the trade-mirroring feature described in Section 4, where enabled on an account.
Law enforcement / regulatorsWhere we are legally required to disclose data, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

We do not sell your personal data.

7.Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your session. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how the Platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the Platform from working.

8.International transfers

Some of our service providers (including Supabase and Stripe) may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on the providers' own adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses to keep your data protected to a UK-GDPR-equivalent standard.

9.How long we keep data

We keep account and trading data for as long as your account is active, and for a further period afterwards where needed for legal, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes. KYC documents and payment records are retained for a minimum of 5 years from the end of our relationship with you, consistent with our anti-money-laundering obligations. As described in our Terms, Trading Data may be retained and used in anonymised/aggregated form indefinitely.

10.Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and access controls restricting who inside IPFX can view trading and account data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

11.Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure of your data; object to or restrict certain processing; request a portable copy of your data; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (including trade mirroring). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. As noted in our Terms, Trading Data retained in anonymised/aggregated form is not personal data and is not subject to erasure requests. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe we have mishandled your data.

12.Children

The Platform is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18. If we learn we've collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be highlighted on the Platform or communicated by email. Continued use of the Platform after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14.Contact us

For any question about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact us at enquiries@ipfxcapital.com.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions. If any part of this policy conflicts with the Terms regarding data rights, the Terms govern unless applicable data protection law says otherwise.
