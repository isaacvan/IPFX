4.6 Infinity Challenge — Detailed Programme Rules

The Infinity Challenge is a four-stage skill evaluation programme. Entry is free of charge. The following rules are exact, mandatory, and non-negotiable. 4.6.1 Stage 1 — Evaluation ($1,000 Virtual Capital)

Stage 1 is the initial skill filter. The Participant trades a simulated $1,000 account subject to all of the following rules simultaneously — all rules apply at all times and there are no exceptions:

  • Profit Target: The Participant must achieve a minimum net profit of 4% ($40) to complete Stage 1. Profit is calculated on closed trades only.
  • Daily Loss Limit: The Participant must not allow account equity or balance to fall by more than 2.5% ($25) in any single calendar day (00:00–23:59 server time).
  • Trailing Drawdown Limit: The Participant must not allow a trailing drawdown exceeding 5% ($50), measured from the account's all-time equity peak.
  • Observation Period: At least 14 elapsed calendar days and 10 meaningful trading days are required.
  • Independent Exposure Sessions: At least 30 sessions are required. Overlapping positions and entries opened within 60 minutes of the preceding return to flat count as one session, regardless of ticket count. In plain terms, a session is one burst of trading. Example: you buy at 09:00, add a second ticket at 09:10, close both at 09:40, then open and close another trade at 10:20 to 10:30: that is one session; a trade opened at 11:45 (more than 60 minutes after you were last flat) starts a second session.
  • Profit Concentration: The best trading day may not exceed 35% of total positive daily profit.
  • Maximum Risk per Trade: A stop-loss is mandatory. Base risk may not exceed 0.50% of starting balance ($5), and the live limit can reduce further under clause 7.7.
  • Daily Profit Cap: New orders are blocked after 1.4% ($14) net daily profit. The cap prevents one day from dominating the evaluation; losses continue to count in full.
  • Maximum Attempts: A Participant may start Stage 1 once per calendar month at no cost. A failed Stage 1 attempt cannot be restarted in the same calendar month; another attempt may be requested from the first day of the following calendar month, subject to eligibility and approval.

  • Profitable Days: At least 40% of trading days must qualify as profitable under clause 7.5. For accounts opened from 1 October 2026, the day's net closed profit must exceed 0.25% of the starting balance ($12.50 in Stage 2).
  • Profit Concentration: The best trading day may not exceed 25% of total positive daily profit.
  • Maximum Risk per Trade: 0.35% of starting balance ($17.50) per trade. A stop-loss is mandatory and the live limit can reduce further under clause 7.7.

4.6.6 Stop-loss warnings and failed-account lockout

Keep a valid stop-loss on every Infinity position. If a position remains without one after 30 seconds, it is closed automatically. Positive profit from that trade is removed; losses remain. Strikes reset on a new approved run, but the next-month lockout in clause 4.6.5 still applies. A separate team warning is a notice and does not create a second strike for the same event.

Touching or falling below either loss floor can fail the account on the first usable persisted equity observation. Equity includes unrealised positions using executable bid/ask prices. Intraday trailing drawdown follows the observed equity peak; daily calculations use UTC and the applicable account’s saved rule version. A price recovery does not undo a breach. Protective closes continue, and order attempts show the failure and next eligibility date. Technical failures, missing prices or execution delay do not guarantee an exact exit at a stop or loss floor.

7.7 Lot Size and Position Limits

Dynamic risk protection. The maximum risk per trade shown for a programme is a base cap, not a guaranteed allowance. At order entry and whenever a stop-loss is changed, the Platform applies the lower of: (a) the programme's base cap; (b) 20% of the remaining maximum-drawdown room; and (c) 25% of the remaining daily-loss room. A trade that would exceed the resulting live limit is rejected. This prevents a Participant from using the remaining loss allowance as a single gamble while leaving normal-sized trades unaffected when the account is comfortably within its limits.

For Evaluation Accounts opened on or after 1 October 2026 on programmes with a maximum risk per trade, the combined risk of all open positions (measured from each position's entry price to its stop-loss) may not exceed three times that per-trade limit. Orders that would exceed it are rejected.

7.10 Order Execution and Trading Costs

The Platform is a simulated trading environment. To reflect real market conditions, and to prevent exploitation of price delivery latency, the following apply to Evaluation Accounts opened on or after 1 October 2026 and to any Funded Account arising from them:

  • Execution delay. Market orders and closes you initiate are executed after a processing delay of up to one second. The execution price is the less favourable to you of the price when your order was received and the price at the moment of execution.
  • Slippage. Market orders, closes you initiate, stop-loss fills and triggered stop orders are subject to slippage at the rate set for each instrument, currently up to 0.3 basis points for forex, 0.3 to 0.5 basis points for CME futures, 0.5 basis points for metals and indices, and 5 basis points for cryptocurrencies. Take-profit and limit orders fill at their stated price or better.
  • Commission. A commission is charged per lot when a position, or part of a position, is closed: currently US$6 per standard lot round-turn on forex and metals, US$4 per full-size contract and US$1 per micro contract round-turn on CME futures, and none on indices and cryptocurrencies. Commission is deducted from the trade's profit or added to its loss.
  • Stops are not guaranteed. If the market moves through your stop level, including across a market gap, your position is closed at the next available price, which may be worse than the stop level.
  • Off-market prices. Prices that deviate abnormally from the prevailing market may be disregarded, and orders may be rejected while a reliable price is unavailable.

Execution costs are recorded on every trade and shown in your trade history. The rates above may be changed in accordance with Section 18.
